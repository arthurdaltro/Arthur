Transformation Center/start redesign · before & after
Try the new /start

One form. A clear next step. Same prayerful matching.

The new /start sits in front of the practice system you already use (SimplePractice). It adds a safety check, clear prices, service and language choice, and lets simple requests book right away. Everything else still goes to your team for a personal, prayerful match.

Today

4 hand-offs before a booking

  1. Interest form (contact details only)
  2. Wait for email #1 with the intake link
  3. Intake paperwork in the client portal
  4. Wait 1–3 business days for matching
  5. Wait for email #2 with the booking link
  6. Book an appointment
New /start

1 form, then book now or a personal match

  1. Safety check first (988 / 911 if needed)
  2. Choose a service, with price and format visible
  3. Language, age, discount, optional preferred minister
  4. Contact details and consent
  5. Simple fit → book right away in the practice system
  6. Everything else → prayerful match, with a clear timeline (3 business days)

Built-in rules

  • Never diagnose or promise outcomes
  • Only asks what routing needs; no medical details
  • Anything the system can't decide goes to the team, never a silent default
  • Same input gives the same routing (17 automated tests)

To connect it for real

  • Paste the SimplePractice booking-widget URL into .env
  • Fill in each minister's languages, formats and services in catalog.json
  • Optional: add Sheets and Resend keys for an intake log and emails
  • Verify prices and languages against the live pages
